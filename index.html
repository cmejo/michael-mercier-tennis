Community & Leadership

A Lifetime of Service to New England Tennis

Throughout his distinguished career, Michael Mercier has been a cornerstone of New England's athletic community. In his two decades at Harvard University, Michael provided individual, group, and private instruction to over 100,000 alumni, students, family members, and affiliates.

Beyond collegiate athletics, Michael has dedicated countless hours to volunteer initiatives and adaptive sports, including leadership roles with:

  • Tenacity: Empowering urban youth through tennis and academic enrichment.
  • Special Olympics New England: Bringing the joy and fitness of tennis to athletes of all abilities.
  • Wheelchair Tennis Programs: Expanding adaptive sports access across Greater Boston and the North Shore.
  • Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital: Utilizing tennis stroke mechanics in therapeutic rehabilitation programs.
  • USPTA New England Division: Serving as Division Treasurer and mentoring upcoming tennis coaches.

Today, based in Beverly, MA, Michael brings this wealth of world-class knowledge, patience, and contagious enthusiasm to players year-round across the North Shore and Southern New Hampshire.
