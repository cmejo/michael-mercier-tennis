Biography & Career History

Michael Louis Mercier: A Legacy of Championship Coaching

USPTA Certified Master Professional, 20-Year Head Tennis Professional at Harvard University, and Hall of Fame Inductee.

Harvard University Recreation & Athletics

"Michael Mercier provides individual, group, and private instruction for over 100,000 Harvard alumni, family, and affiliates. He volunteers and serves as a leader for Harvard community programs including Harvard Men/Women Club Tennis, Tenacity, Special Olympics, Wheelchair Tennis, Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital, and racquet stringing operations." — Harvard Athletics Bio

Two Decades Guiding Tennis at Harvard University

For over 20 years, Michael Mercier served as the Head Tennis Professional at Harvard University in Cambridge, Massachusetts. During his tenure at Harvard, Michael was the guiding force behind the Murr Center and Beren Tennis Center instructional programming. He worked directly with undergraduate scholars, graduate researchers, distinguished faculty, and Harvard alumni, creating an inclusive, high-energy environment that introduced thousands of people to the lifelong sport of tennis while honing the competitive skills of collegiate club athletes.

His role encompassed not only world-class technical coaching, but also running large-scale clinic structures, coordinating university tournaments, and overseeing high-precision racquet customization and stringing for competitive players.

The Elite USPTA Master Professional Credential

The title of USPTA Master Professional is the ultimate accreditation recognized in international tennis coaching. Fewer than one percent (1%) of certified professionals worldwide achieve this distinction. To become a Master Professional, a coach must demonstrate decades of proven player development, publish instructional methodologies, conduct continuing education seminars, and show extraordinary leadership in racquet sports.

Michael's dedication to the craft has also earned him:

  • USPTA New England Hall of Fame Inductee: Honoring his long-term impact on the New England tennis landscape.
  • USPTA Lifetime Achievement Award: Recognizing decades of mentoring coaches and expanding grass-roots tennis.
  • USPTA New England Division Treasurer: Serving on the executive board to manage regional coach education and events.

The Gardner Ward Chase Memorial Award from USTA New England

One of the crowning achievements of Michael's career was receiving the Gardner Ward Chase Memorial Award from the United States Tennis Association (USTA) New England.

The Gardner Ward Chase Memorial Award is the single highest honor bestowed by USTA New England. It is presented to an individual who has made outstanding lifetime contributions to the game of tennis throughout New England. Nominees must possess an extraordinary passion for tennis, inspire others across generations, and dedicate themselves selflessly to growing the game.

Commitment to Community & Adaptive Sports

Michael’s philosophy has always centered on using racquet sports to enrich lives. Throughout his career, he has been a tireless advocate and volunteer for adaptive sports programs across Massachusetts:

  • Special Olympics Massachusetts: Designing specialized ball-control and agility drills tailored to athletes with intellectual disabilities.
  • Wheelchair Tennis: Helping athletes with spinal injuries and mobility impairments master court movement and racquet leverage.
  • Spaulding Rehabilitation Hospital: Integrating racquet sports mechanics into patient physical recovery protocols.
  • Tenacity Boston: Mentoring at-risk urban youth through tennis instruction and academic support.

Private Coaching in Beverly & The North Shore

Now residing in Beverly, MA, Michael offers his 30+ years of expertise to junior athletes, high school competitors, adult league champions, and passionate beginners across the North Shore of Massachusetts and Southern New Hampshire.

With the explosion of pickleball, Michael has also integrated high-level pickleball instruction into his curriculum. Combining tennis biomechanics with nuanced kitchen-line strategy, he helps players master the soft touch, third-shot drops, and fast-hands resets that define top-tier tournament play.
