World-Class Coaching in the Historic Coastal City of Newburyport

Situated at the mouth of the Merrimack River where it empties into the Atlantic Ocean, Newburyport is celebrated for its historic maritime beauty, vibrant downtown waterfront, and active, athletic community. Tennis and pickleball thrive here, with players frequenting riverside courts and residential properties throughout the warmer months.

For Newburyport athletes looking to break through frustrating scoring plateaus and master sound stroke mechanics, Michael Mercier brings an unparalleled coaching background. As a USPTA Certified Master Professional (achieved by fewer than 1% of tennis coaches worldwide) and Head Tennis Professional at Harvard University for more than two decades, Michael provides ivy-league caliber player development directly to Newburyport’s courts.

Where We Train in Newburyport, MA

Private instruction takes place at Newburyport’s top waterfront parks, school courts, and private residential estates:

  • Cashman Park (Sally Snyder Way): Nestled directly along the Merrimack River waterfront, Cashman Park features popular municipal tennis courts with scenic river views and refreshing summer breezes. It is an inspiring venue for morning technical drills, footwork conditioning, and live-ball baseline rallies.
  • Perkins Park (Beacon Avenue): A picturesque neighborhood park offering quiet, well-maintained courts tucked away in Newburyport’s North End, perfect for unhurried 1-on-1 stroke reconstruction, video analysis, and serve practice.
  • Newburyport High School Athletic Courts: Home to the Newburyport Clippers varsity tennis program, these courts provide regulation surfaces for junior players preparing for Cape Ann League (CAL) varsity tryouts and USTA New England junior tournaments.
  • Private Residential Courts: Along High Street, Moseley Woods, and the Plum Island corridor, several families have private backyard tennis or pickleball courts. Michael travels directly to your residence with equipment, ball carts, and video analysis tools for private, customized coaching.

Tennis Biomechanics: Power, Depth & Repeatable Motion

Many players in Newburyport find that their groundstrokes lack penetration or that their serve breaks down during tight third sets. Michael’s coaching focuses on kinetic chain efficiency:

  • The Modern Heavy Topspin Forehand: Synchronizing footwork setup, hip coiling, and forearm pronation to create heavy topspin that jumps up off the court and pins opponents behind the baseline.
  • Continental Grip Serve Mechanics: Replacing flat "pancake" pushes with fluid upward pronation, producing biting slice serves out wide and consistent kick serves that eliminate double faults.
  • Doubles Positioning & Poaching: Teaching adult league doubles partners how to dominate the middle, communicate seamlessly, and execute poaches that rattle opposing returners.

Pickleball in Newburyport: Mastering the Kitchen Game

With pickleball participation growing rapidly across Newburyport, players are eager to move beyond baseline rallying into tactical, high-percentage doubles play. Coach Mike's instruction focuses on:

  • The Third Shot Drop: Mastering paddle face angle and soft grip tension to float gentle, unattackable drops into the kitchen from deep behind the baseline.
  • Kitchen Line Discipline: Developing patient cross-court dinking, learning to reset fast speedups, and waiting for the right ball to attack.
  • Doubles Stacking and Communication: Establishing clear coverage protocols to eliminate hesitation on middle balls.
