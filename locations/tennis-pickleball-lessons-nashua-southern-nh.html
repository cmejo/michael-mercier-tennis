Elite Racquet Coaching in Nashua and the Southern NH Region

As the economic and cultural anchor of Southern New Hampshire, Nashua and its neighboring communities—including Hollis, Hudson, Amherst, and Bedford—boast a thriving tennis tradition and an exploding pickleball community. Ambitious juniors, high school competitors, and adult league players throughout Hillsborough County and the Merrimack Valley are constantly looking for specialized coaching to sharpen their skills.

For Southern New Hampshire players, Michael Mercier brings credentials rarely accessible outside major university athletic departments. Having served as Head Tennis Professional at Harvard University for more than 20 years, coached over 100,000 players, and earned the USPTA Master Professional accreditation (held by fewer than 1% of tennis coaches worldwide), Michael provides high-performance instruction that yields immediate, lasting breakthroughs.

Where We Train in Nashua & Southern New Hampshire

Private instruction takes place at premier public parks, school athletic complexes, and private residential courts:

  • Greeley Park (Concord Street, Nashua): Nashua’s historic 125-acre civic park features classic municipal tennis courts nestled beneath stately pines along the Merrimack River corridor. The serene, shaded park setting makes it an ideal venue for intensive technical drills, video stroke analysis, and serve practice.
  • Nashua High School South Complex (Riverside Street): Home to the Nashua South Panthers tennis teams, this multi-court hard-court facility provides tournament-quality surfaces.   • Pennichuck Middle School Courts: Located on Manchester Street, offering community courts ideal for adult clinics and private technical tune-ups.
  • Private Residential Courts across Southern NH: In affluent residential enclaves throughout Hollis, Amherst, Bedford, and Hudson, many families have private backyard tennis or pickleball courts. Michael travels directly to your home with ball carts, training equipment, and video analysis tools for customized, white-glove coaching on your schedule.

Tennis Biomechanics: Power, Depth & Repeatable Motion

Many players in Southern New Hampshire experience inconsistent groundstrokes or painful shoulder tightness because they "arm" the ball rather than engaging the entire body. Michael teaches modern kinetic chain principles:

  • The Modern Heavy Topspin Forehand: Coordinating lower-body loading, core coiling, and forearm pronation to hit balls that clear the net with heavy topspin and jump off the court with pace.
  • Continental Grip Serve Mechanics: Moving away from "pancake" flat serves to fluid upward pronation, creating sharp slice out wide and kick serves that bounce out of an opponent’s strike zone.
  • Doubles Poaching & Net Strategy: Teaching adult league players how to read server tosses, anticipate returns, and finish volleys crisply at the net.

Pickleball in Southern NH: Mastering the 3rd Shot Drop & NVZ Strategy

The pickleball scene across Nashua and Southern New Hampshire is expanding rapidly, but players often hit a plateau around the 3.0 or 3.5 rating. Michael’s pickleball coaching zeroes in on the nuances of high-level play:

  • The Third Shot Drop: Mastering paddle face angle and soft grip tension needed to land the ball gently in the kitchen from deep behind the baseline.
  • Kitchen Line Discipline: Developing calm, patient cross-court dinking, learning to reset fast speedups, and waiting for attackable balls.
  • Doubles Stacking and Court Coverage: Coordinating with your partner on middle-court communication and coverage to eliminate confusion.
