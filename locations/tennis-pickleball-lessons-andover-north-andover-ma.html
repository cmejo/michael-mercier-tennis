Tennis Biomechanics: Power, Depth & Match Consistency

Michael’s coaching methodology focuses on unlocking natural athleticism through biomechanics rather than forcing unnatural, stiff mechanics:

  • The Modern Kinetic Forehand: Synchronizing footwork setup, hip coiling, and forearm pronation to hit heavy topspin balls that explode off the court with depth and pace.
  • First & Second Serve Biomechanics: Developing a fluid continental grip, tossing consistency, trophy pose, and upward leg drive to add 10–15 MPH while eliminating shoulder strain.
  • Mental Toughness & Point Construction: Drawing on two decades of coaching Ivy League collegiate athletes to teach juniors how to manage match nerves, construct high-percentage points, and close out tight tiebreakers.

Pickleball in the Andovers: Transitioning to 4.0+ Tournament Level

Pickleball popularity across Andover and North Andover is booming, but players often hit a frustrating wall when competing against experienced tournament players. Michael’s pickleball coaching targets:

  • The Third Shot Drop: Mastering soft hands, paddle angle, and knee flexion to drop the ball safely into the opponent’s kitchen from behind the baseline.
  • Kitchen Line Discipline: Developing calm cross-court dinking, learning to reset fast speedups, and waiting patiently for an attackable floating ball.
  • Doubles Stacking and Communication: Establishing clear coverage protocols to eliminate hesitation on middle balls.
