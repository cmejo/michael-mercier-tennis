Tennis Instruction: Modern Biomechanics & Injury Prevention

Many adult league players in Salem struggle with recurrent forearm fatigue, shoulder strain, or an inconsistent second serve. In your private sessions, Michael evaluates your technique using slow-motion video analysis to pinpoint exact leverage issues:

  • The Modern Open-Stance Forehand: Replacing arm-driven swings with hip coil, core uncoiling, and wrist lag that maximizes topspin clearance over the net.
  • High-Percentage Serve Placement: Developing a reliable kick and slice serve using continental grip pronation rather than forceful, flat pushing that risks rotator cuff injury.
  • Doubles Poaching & Net Aggression: Positioning yourself to dominate the middle in North Shore and USTA New England league doubles.

Pickleball in Salem: Transitioning from Casual to Competitive

Pickleball courts in Salem are buzzing, but winning consistently requires shifting away from high-risk smash games toward nuanced soft-game control. Coach Mike guides players through:

  • Soft Hands at the Kitchen Line: Mastering the non-volley zone (NVZ) push-dink and absorbing heavy drives off the bounce.
  • The 3rd Shot Drop Routine: Developing the muscle memory and knee bend necessary to drop the ball softly into the opponent's kitchen from deep behind the baseline.
  • Court Position Awareness: Learning how to avoid "no-man's land" in transition and effectively stack with your doubles partner.
